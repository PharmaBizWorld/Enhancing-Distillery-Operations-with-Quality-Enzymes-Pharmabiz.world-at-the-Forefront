The Role of Enzymes in Distillery Operations
Enzymes are biological catalysts that significantly enhance the efficiency of various stages in distillation. From starch conversion to fermentation, enzymes play a critical role in optimizing processes, improving yields, and ensuring product consistency. Key enzymes such as amylases, glucoamylases, and proteases aid in breaking down complex carbohydrates and proteins into fermentable sugars, providing a foundation for high-quality alcohol production.
